# Experimental Approaches Archive

This directory contains planning documents for approaches that were explored but either superseded by better solutions or deferred for future consideration.

## Purpose

Documents here represent:
- **Experimental designs** - Approaches that were tried but not adopted
- **Superseded patterns** - Solutions replaced by simpler or more robust alternatives
- **Deferred ideas** - Valid approaches postponed for later phases

## Why Keep Experimental Docs?

1. **Avoid repeating mistakes**: Documents why certain approaches didn't work
2. **Future reference**: Ideas may become relevant in different contexts
3. **Learning value**: Shows evolution of architectural thinking
4. **Credit ideas**: Acknowledges exploration work even if not shipped
